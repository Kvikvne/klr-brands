Step 1

Logging in

The admin panel is only accessible to you — buyers never see it.

01
Go to the login page

Navigate to your site URL and add /login at the end. Enter your email and password.

02
You'll land on the admin dashboard

You'll see a summary of active campaigns, total orders, and recent activity. The left sidebar has links to every section.

Step 2

Creating a campaign

A campaign is a single group order — one storefront link, one deadline, one set of products. Create a new one from Admin → Campaigns → New Campaign.

Field What to put here
Title required The name shown at the top of the store — e.g. "Spring 2025 Tees"
URL slug required The short word that goes in the link — lowercase, no spaces, hyphens okay. E.g. spring-2025 → your link becomes /store/spring-2025
Description optional A short blurb that appears on the storefront — pickup details, deadline reminders, anything useful for buyers
Deadline optional The last day buyers can place orders. Shown on the storefront. Defaults to two weeks from today.
Tip

You can change the title, description, and deadline later — but changing the slug after you've shared the link will break it.

Step 3

Adding products and mockup images

After creating the campaign you'll be taken to its detail page. Scroll down to the Products section and click Add product.

01
Pick a product from the list

The list shows everything in your catalog. Click the one you want to add to this campaign.

02
Select which colors to offer

Check all the color options you want buyers to be able to choose from. You must pick at least one.

03
Upload a mockup image (optional, but recommended)

This is a photo or mockup of the finished item that buyers will see on the store. JPEG, PNG, or WebP — up to 10 MB. You can skip this and upload it later using the "Upload" button on the product card.

04
Click "Add to campaign"

The product appears in the list. Repeat for every item in this campaign.

After adding

Each product card lets you replace the mockup image, edit which colors are offered, or remove the product entirely — all without touching any other products.

Banner image

The Banner image section (above Products) is a single wide image shown at the top of the storefront — a flat-lay, lifestyle photo, or general campaign image. It's optional.

Step 4

Publishing and sharing the link

New campaigns start as a Draft — invisible to buyers. When you're ready, change the status to Live in the Details section and save.

Your store link

Once live, share this link with your group:

yourdomain.com/store/your-slug

Buyers don't need an account. They open the link, pick their items, and check out.

Note

Setting the status back to Draft hides the store immediately and any partially filled carts will be lost. Only go live when you're ready to start collecting orders.

Step 5

Viewing and managing orders

Orders come in as buyers check out. You can see them in two places:

Admin → Orders
All orders, grouped by campaign
Campaign detail page
Orders for that campaign only

Each order card shows the buyer's name, email, phone, fulfillment preference (pickup or delivery), any delivery address and notes, and a breakdown of everything they ordered with the per-item price.

Changing an order's status

Each order card has four status buttons — click any one to instantly move that order to that status. See the next section for what each status means.

Step 6

Order statuses explained

Each order moves through a simple pipeline. You control the status manually — use it to track where you are in the fulfillment process.

PENDING Just placed. Buyer can still cancel via their email link.
→
CONFIRMED You've ordered materials. Buyer can no longer cancel.
→
FULFILLED Order has been picked up or delivered.
CANCELLED

An order can be cancelled from any status — either by the buyer (if still Pending, via the link in their confirmation email) or by you at any point. Cancelled orders stay visible in the list for your records.

When to mark Confirmed

Do this once you've placed your order for shirt blanks or print materials. It signals to the platform that the order is locked — the buyer's cancel link will stop working.

Step 7

Bulk actions

At the top of the orders section on any campaign page you'll find two shortcut buttons to move orders in bulk — no need to click each card one by one.

Confirm all pending

Moves every PENDING order to CONFIRMED at once. Use this when you've placed your materials order and want to lock everything down in one go. The button shows how many orders will be affected.

Fulfill all confirmed

Moves every CONFIRMED order to FULFILLED at once. Use this after a pickup event or once you've finished delivering everything.

Heads up

Both buttons apply immediately with no undo. Double-check before clicking — especially "Confirm all pending," since it will lock buyers out of cancelling.

Step 8

Exporting orders to CSV

Click Export CSV in the bulk actions bar of any campaign to download a spreadsheet you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

—
Materials needed (top section)

A totalled list of every product, color, and size combination across all orders — exactly what you need to place your materials order. One row per variant with the total quantity.

—
Full order list (bottom section)

Every order in one row — buyer name, email, phone, status, fulfillment type, delivery address, notes, and all items summarized in a single column.

Tip

Export again at any time — it always reflects the current state of orders, including any status changes you've made since the last export.

Step 9

Deleting a campaign

The delete button is in the top-right corner of any campaign's detail page.

This is permanent

Deleting a campaign removes it and every order associated with it — permanently, with no way to recover. If the campaign has existing orders, the confirmation dialog will tell you exactly how many will be deleted. Export your CSV first if you need a record.

Campaigns that are simply finished don't need to be deleted — setting the status to Fulfilled or Closed is enough. Deletion is for campaigns created by mistake or ones you want completely removed from your records.
